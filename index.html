Aim Trainer

Passthrough alignment drill for Quest 3

  1. Clear ~4m of floor space. Passthrough stays on — you'll see your real room.
  2. Right controller trigger: place the ball at your feet, then place the target ring across the room.
  3. Take your normal golf stance over the ball. Hold trigger and drag on the floor to lay your alignment stick where you think the target line runs. Release to lock it.
  4. The app shows your aim error in degrees and reveals the true line.
  5. Squeeze grip: new attempt (same ball & target). Hold grip 1.5s: full reset.
Point at the floor by your feet and pull the trigger to place the ball.
