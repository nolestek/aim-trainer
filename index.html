Aim Trainer

Passthrough alignment drill for Quest 3

  1. Clear ~4m of floor. Passthrough stays on — you'll see your real room.
  2. Trigger drops the ball at your feet, then sets the target across the room.
  3. Over the ball, hold trigger and drag a line the way you think you're aimed. Release to lock.
  4. You get your aim error in degrees, plus your average bias across all attempts.
  5. Grip = step back one stage (move the target, redo the ball, or go again) · hold grip 1.5s = full reset.
Point at the floor by your feet and pull the trigger to place the ball.
